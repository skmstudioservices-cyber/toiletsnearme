🗺 The fastest ways to find a toilet nearby

Open Google Maps and search "public toilet near me", "restroom near me" or "Sulabh toilet near me". Results usually include distance, opening hours and user ratings.

If nothing shows up, search for nearby landmarks instead: "petrol pump", "metro station", "railway station", "bus stand", "mall" or "hotel". All of these have restrooms.

🇮🇳 Public toilets in India — what to expect

1. Free community / public toilets

Built and maintained by municipal bodies. Free or nominally priced, found near markets, bus stands and stations.

2. Pay-and-use toilets (Sulabh and similar)

A small fee (typically ₹5–₹10) gets you a cleaner, attended facility with water and soap.

3. Facility toilets (malls, metros, petrol pumps)

Often the cleanest and safest option, especially on highways and in city centres.

👩 Women, children & accessibility

  • Many community toilets in Hyderabad have separate women's sections — look for "Mahila" signage.
  • Swachh Bharat guidelines require accessible toilets for persons with disabilities in public facilities.
  • At night, prefer lit, attended facilities inside petrol pumps, hotels or 24×7 chains.

❓ Public toilets in Hyderabad — FAQ

How do I find a public toilet in Hyderabad right now?

Use the live map on this page, or search 'public toilet near me' in Google Maps. Petrol pumps, metro stations and malls are reliable fallbacks.

Are public toilets in Hyderabad free?

Many community toilets are free or cost a token amount. Pay-and-use toilets typically charge ₹5–₹10.

Are there separate toilets for women in Hyderabad?

Yes — most public and pay-and-use toilets have separate women's sections, often marked 'Mahila'.

Are wheelchair-accessible toilets available?

Swachh Bharat guidelines require accessible facilities in public toilets. Use the ♿ filter on the map above.

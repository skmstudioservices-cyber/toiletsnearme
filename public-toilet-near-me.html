❓ Frequently asked questions

Are public toilets in India free?

Many community toilets are free; pay-and-use ones (Sulabh) typically charge ₹5–₹10 and are usually cleaner.

Which public toilets are open at night?

24×7 petrol-pump washrooms, metro stations and airport toilets are the most reliable after dark.

How do I find the nearest toilet fast?

Open Google Maps and search 'public toilet near me', or use the live map on this page to see options with distance.

Are there separate toilets for women?

Yes — most public and pay-and-use toilets have separate women's sections, often marked 'Mahila', usually with attendants.

Are accessible toilets available?

Large malls, airports and metro stations usually have wheelchair-accessible toilets; smaller roadside ones often do not.

Do I need an app?

No — Google Maps is the most complete in India. Searching 'public toilet' or 'restroom' is enough.

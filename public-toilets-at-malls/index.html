Public toilets at malls — the dependable free option

Page last updated 29 September 2026

Every Indian mall keeps clean, free, staffed restrooms on multiple floors — no purchase required. Here is how to find them fast.

Quick answerMall restrooms are free, cleaned hourly and open all mall hours — usually on every floor near the food court and lifts, and no purchase is needed to use them. Find the nearest mall via Google Maps (“mall near me”) or check our live map for nearby public toilet options. Most Indian malls also have wheelchair-accessible stalls.

Why malls are the best free option

  • Attendants clean through the day — not once in the morning
  • Water, soap and usually toilet paper or jet sprays
  • Security and lighting make them safe at any hour the mall is open
  • Accessible stalls and baby-changing in most centres

Finding the restroom inside

Restrooms cluster near food courts, cinema entrances and lift lobbies — typically one set per floor. Directory boards mark them, and any security guard will point you to the nearest one.

Hours and etiquette

Restrooms open with the mall and shut with it — usually 10 am to 10–11 pm. As a non-shopper, walking in to use the restroom is normal and accepted across Indian malls.

❓ Public toilets at malls — FAQ

Can I use a mall toilet without buying anything?

Yes — mall restrooms are public facilities during mall hours; no purchase is required.

Are mall toilets free?

Yes, in Indian malls restrooms are free and cleaned by attendants through the day.

Where inside the mall are the toilets?

Usually on every floor near the food court, cinema entrance or lift lobby — check the directory boards or ask security.

What time do mall toilets close?

With the mall — commonly 10 am to around 10–11 pm. Outside those hours, hospitals and railway stations are the open options.

Do malls have accessible toilets?

Most modern Indian malls have wheelchair-accessible stalls and baby-changing rooms — see our accessible toilets guide.

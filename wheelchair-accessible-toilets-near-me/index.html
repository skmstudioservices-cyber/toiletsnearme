Wheelchair accessible toilets near me — where they are and what to expect

Page last updated 29 September 2026

India's accessible-restroom coverage is improving fast in new buildings — knowing where to look makes all the difference.

Quick answerThe most dependable wheelchair-accessible toilets: malls, airports, metro stations and newer pay-and-use complexes. Look for the wheelchair symbol on directories; Indian law (the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016) requires accessible toilets in public buildings. Our live map shows toilet data where accessibility is recorded.

Where accessible toilets concentrate

  • Malls and multiplexes: accessible stalls on every restroom floor — the most consistent option.
  • Airports and metro stations: designed to modern accessibility standards.
  • Newer pay-and-use complexes: many include at least one accessible cubicle.
  • Hospitals: accessible restrooms near OPD and emergency entrances.

What makes a toilet accessible

A wheelchair-accessible toilet needs: a wide door, enough turning space, grab bars beside the seat, a lower seat and washbasin, and an emergency call facility where possible. A wheelchair symbol on the door is the signal to look for.

Your legal rights

The RPwD Act 2016 mandates accessibility in public buildings, including restrooms. Accessible India Campaign audits push compliance — complaints about a specific building can be raised with the local disability commissioner.

❓ Wheelchair accessible toilets near me — FAQ

Where can I find a wheelchair accessible toilet near me?

Malls, airports, metro stations, hospitals and newer pay-and-use complexes are the most reliable; look for the wheelchair symbol on directory boards.

Are accessible toilets free in India?

Where the facility is free (malls, hospitals, most stations), the accessible stall is free too; pay-and-use complexes do not charge extra for accessible cubicles.

What does the RPwD Act say about toilets?

The Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016 requires public buildings to provide accessible facilities, including restrooms, with the Accessible India Campaign driving compliance.

What features make a toilet accessible?

Wide doors, turning space for a wheelchair, grab bars, a lower seat and basin, and ideally an emergency call button.

Do railway stations have accessible toilets?

Major stations have at least one accessible toilet, typically near the main entrance or waiting hall — station staff can direct you.

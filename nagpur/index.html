🗺 The fastest ways to find a toilet nearby

Google Maps is the most reliable tool in India. Open the app or website and search "public toilet near me", "restroom near me" or "Sulabh toilet near me". Results usually include:

  • Distance and walking time from your current location
  • Opening hours (many public toilets close at night)
  • User ratings and photos — the fastest way to judge cleanliness

If nothing shows up, search for nearby landmarks instead: "petrol pump", "metro station", "railway station", "bus stand", "mall" or "hotel". All of these have restrooms, and most allow non-customers to use them, sometimes for a small fee.

🇮🇳 Public toilets in India — what to expect

India has expanded public sanitation massively under the Swachh Bharat Mission. You will generally find three kinds of facilities:

1. Free community / public toilets

Built and maintained by municipal bodies. Free or nominally priced, found near markets, bus stands and slum-adjacent areas. Cleanliness varies — check recent Google Maps reviews.

2. Pay-and-use toilets (Sulabh and similar)

Operated by NGOs and private agencies — a small fee (typically ₹5, sometimes ₹10) gets you a cleaner, attended facility with water and soap. Sulabh International operates thousands across India.

3. Facility toilets (malls, metros, petrol pumps)

Often the cleanest and safest option. Malls and metro stations have well-maintained restrooms; highway petrol pumps increasingly have food courts and restrooms.

👩 Women, children & accessibility

  • Many community toilets have separate women's sections — look for "Mahila" signage or female attendants.
  • Swachh Bharat guidelines require accessible toilets for persons with disabilities in public facilities.
  • When travelling at night, prefer lit, attended facilities inside petrol pumps, hotels or 24×7 chains rather than dark standalone public toilets.

❓ Public toilets in Nagpur — FAQ

How do I find a public toilet near me right now?

Open Google Maps and search 'public toilet near me' or 'restroom near me'. You'll see the nearest options with hours and photos. Petrol pumps, metro stations and malls are reliable fallbacks.

Are public toilets in India free?

Many community toilets are free or cost a token amount. Pay-and-use toilets (like Sulabh facilities) typically charge ₹5–₹10 and are usually cleaner.

Which apps show public toilets?

Google Maps is the most complete in India. Simply search 'public toilet' or 'restroom' — no special app is needed.

Are there separate toilets for women?

Yes — most public and pay-and-use toilets have separate women's sections, often marked 'Mahila', and many have female attendants.

24 hour toilet near me — what is actually open at night

Most public toilets in India close in the evening. This page covers the facilities that genuinely stay open overnight, and how to check before you rely on one.

The facilities that stay open

  • Petrol pumps — the single most reliable overnight option, especially on highways.
  • Hospitals — open 24×7 and never turn anyone away.
  • Airports and major railway stations — open through the night.
  • 24×7 convenience stores and large restaurant chains.

Why standalone public toilets close

  • Most municipal toilets are staffed and locked overnight for safety and maintenance.
  • Pay-and-use complexes vary — some run 24 hours near stations, most do not.

How to check before you go

  • OpenStreetMap carries opening_hours tags for many facilities. Our city maps show those tags where they exist.
  • A missing tag means not recorded, not closed — treat it as unknown and have a fallback ready.

Frequently asked questions

Are there 24 hour public toilets in India?

Yes, but they are mostly at petrol pumps, hospitals, airports and major railway stations rather than standalone municipal toilets.

What is the safest toilet option at night?

An attended, well-lit facility — petrol pump, hospital lobby or a 24×7 store — rather than a dark standalone public toilet.

Do your maps show opening hours?

Where OpenStreetMap has the tag, yes. A missing tag means it is not recorded yet, not that the facility is closed.

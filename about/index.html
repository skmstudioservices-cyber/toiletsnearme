About Toilets Near Me

Updated 29 September 2026

🎯 Why this site exists

Finding a working charger or a clean public toilet in an unfamiliar Indian city is still harder than it should be — scattered apps, stale listings, no clear answer on free vs paid or open-now. This site gives you one live map, one clear answer, zero signup.

⚙️ How it works

  • Locations come from OpenStreetMap, refreshed monthly — the same community data used by millions.
  • Tap any pin for details, or use "📍 near me" for instant directions with a DIGIPIN (India's digital address code).
  • Every guide is written for a real situation: road trips, pay-and-use, free options, accessibility, stations and malls.
  • Nothing to install, no account needed.

🤝 Spot something wrong?

Maps improve through people like you. Tap any pin's suggest option, or email skmstudio.services@gmail.com — approved fixes appear after review. You can also edit OpenStreetMap directly; our map picks it up automatically.

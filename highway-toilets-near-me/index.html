Highway toilets near me — clean stops on Indian highways

A long drive in India lives or dies by where you can stop. This page covers the clean washrooms that are actually documented on Indian highways — NHAI wayside amenities, 24x7 petrol pumps and the rest stops worth planning around.

NHAI wayside amenities — the most reliable highway washrooms

  • NHAI has awarded wayside amenities at roughly 501 sites, with around 94 operational, spaced roughly every 40-60 km on major corridors.
  • They combine fuel, food, parking and clean toilets in one place — the single best stop on a long drive.

When there is no wayside amenity

  • Petrol pumps are the fallback — most highway outlets have restrooms, and the larger ones with food courts keep them clean.
  • Toll plazas often have facilities on the approach road.
  • Large dhabas on older highways usually have washrooms for customers.

Planning a stop before you leave

  • Work out your stops in 100-150 km blocks on a highway run; that keeps you within reach of a documented amenity or a large fuel station.
  • At night, prefer an attended, well-lit facility over a standalone roadside toilet.
  • Carry tissue and hand sanitiser — supply at highway facilities is inconsistent.

Frequently asked questions

Are there clean toilets on Indian highways?

Yes — NHAI wayside amenities are the most reliable, with around 94 operational across the network, roughly every 40-60 km on major corridors.

What is the best toilet stop on a long drive in India?

A wayside amenity or a large petrol pump with a food court. Both are attended and usually clean.

Do highway petrol pumps allow non-customers to use the toilet?

In practice, yes at almost all of them. Buying something small is the courteous option.

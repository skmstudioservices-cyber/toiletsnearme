What is reliably open at night

  • Hospitals: emergency departments are open 24×7 and have restrooms nearby.
  • Railway stations: platform facilities never close with the station.
  • Major junction pay-and-use complexes: attendants run night shifts at bus stands and markets.
  • Highway petrol pumps: many stay open 24 hours with restrooms.

Check before you walk

Opening hours change — a mall restroom dies with the mall. Google Maps lists hours for most facilities, and our map pins carry hours where the data records them. A 30-second check beats a locked door.

Safety at night

Prefer lit, staffed locations: hospital lobbies, station concourses and attended pay-and-use complexes over unlit street-side urinals.

❓ Public toilets near me open now — FAQ

Which public toilets are open 24 hours?

Hospital emergency lobbies, railway stations, and 24-hour pay-and-use complexes at major junctions and bus stands are the most reliable round-the-clock options.

Are railway station toilets open at night?

Yes — stations are open around the clock and their facilities follow the station, not office hours.

Do pay and use toilets stay open at night?

Some do, especially those with attendants at bus stands and major markets — check the posted hours or Google Maps listing first.

Where can I find a toilet at 2 am?

The nearest hospital emergency entrance or railway station. Both are staffed and open around the clock.

How do I check if a toilet is open now?

Check the hours on its Google Maps listing, or the pin details on our live map, before setting out.

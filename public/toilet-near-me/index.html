Toilet near me — how to find the nearest one fast

When you need a toilet, the nearest option beats the best option. This page explains how to find one in under a minute in any Indian city, and where they reliably are.

The 60-second method

  • Open Google Maps and search public toilet near me. It returns the nearest mapped facilities with distance, hours and often photos.
  • If nothing appears, search for a landmark instead: petrol pump, metro station, railway station, bus stand, mall, hospital.
  • Every one of those has a restroom, and most allow non-customers to use them.

Where public toilets actually are in an Indian city

  • Municipal community toilets — near markets, bus stands and older residential areas. Free or nominal.
  • Pay-and-use complexes — often near stations and busy markets, ₹5–₹10, attended.
  • Facility toilets — malls, metro stations, petrol pumps, hospitals, large restaurants.
  • Places of worship — larger temples, gurudwaras and mosques usually have facilities.

Frequently asked questions

What is the nearest public toilet to me?

Search public toilet near me in Google Maps for the closest mapped option. Petrol pumps, metro stations and malls are the most reliable fallbacks.

Are public toilets in India free?

Many are — municipal toilets, malls, hospitals and petrol pumps are typically free. Pay-and-use complexes charge ₹5–₹10.

Do Indian cities have 24-hour toilets?

Some do. Petrol pumps and hospitals are the most dependable overnight options; standalone municipal toilets often close at night.

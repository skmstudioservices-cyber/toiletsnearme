What a Sulabh complex is

A Sulabh pay-and-use complex is a staffed public toilet block — separate men's and women's sections, urinals, water supply, and often bathing cubicles. The per-use fee pays the attendant and keeps it cleaned on a schedule.

Fees and passes

Single use typically costs ₹5–10. Regular users — vendors, drivers, daily commuters — can buy inexpensive monthly passes at the complex office.

Finding your nearest complex

  • Our live map shows mapped public toilets with DIGIPIN directions.
  • Google Maps: search “Sulabh toilet near me” or “pay and use toilet”.
  • Look near markets, bus stands, railway stations and hospitals — that is where complexes concentrate.

Beyond Sulabh

Municipal community toilet complexes (CTCs) work the same way in many cities, and mall or hospital restrooms are the free alternatives. See our pay-and-use guide for the full picture.

❓ Sulabh toilets near me — FAQ

What is a Sulabh toilet?

A pay-and-use public toilet complex operated by Sulabh International, with an attendant, water supply and separate sections — usually charging ₹5–10 per use.

How much does a Sulabh toilet cost?

Typically ₹5–10 per visit, with inexpensive monthly passes for regular users.

Are Sulabh toilets clean?

They are among the more dependable public options — the fee funds scheduled cleaning and a full-time attendant.

Do Sulabh complexes have separate facilities for women?

Yes — standard complexes have separate men's and women's sections, and many have bathing cubicles.

Can I get a monthly pass?

Yes — most complexes sell monthly passes at the complex office for vendors and regular users.
